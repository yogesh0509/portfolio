## The problem

A stablecoin is a cryptocurrency meant to stay at a fixed price, such as one US dollar. Djed backs its stablecoin with a reserve of another coin, the basecoin (BC). To price its coins, the contract asks an oracle, a service that reports the basecoin's price. If that price is late or manipulated, people can buy coins for less than they are worth elsewhere, and the reserve pays for the difference.
